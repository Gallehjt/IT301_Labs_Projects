Explore JMU's Campus

JMU has several campus areas, each with different buildings and places to explore. Here are four areas worth getting to know before your first visit.

Bluestone & the Quad

Bluestone is the heart of JMU. The Quad is a popular spot for students to walk through, relax, and hang out with friends.

Look for: Wilson Hall and Carrier Library.

Lakeside

Around Newman Lake, you'll find a different view of campus along with academic and athletics buildings. The lake is an easy landmark to remember.

Look for: Newman Lake, Godwin Hall, and Showker Hall.

Skyline

The Skyline area is located on JMU's east side. Skyline includes science buildings, Rose Library, and the Festival Conference and Student Center, as well as the beautiful Edith J. Carrier Arboretum, a 63-acre botanical garden.

Look for: Rose Library and Festival.

Hillside

Hillside connects you to some of JMU's student gathering spaces. It includes the Grafton-Stovall theater and The Union, a popular area for students to hang out and study.

Look for: The Union and Grafton-Stovall Theatre.

Tip: If you visit JMU, walking around campus can help you get a feel for each area.
