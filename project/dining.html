Food around campus

Dining at JMU

JMU has been ranked No. 3 in the nation for Best Campus Food. Students can enjoy our dining halls or the various food courts and cafes across campus

The Main Dining Halls

D-Hall

Bluestone / West Campus

D-Hall is an all-you-care-to-eat dining hall near the Quad. It offers a range of choices, such as pizza, salads, and rotating hot meals. It also houses a Chick-Fil-A and a Steak-N-Shake below it.

View D-Hall details and menus

E-Hall

Skyline / East Campus

E-Hall is another all-you-care-to-eat dining hall. It's convenient for students spending time on East Campus and includes a variety of stations and meal choices.

View E-Hall details and menus

What About Meal Plans?

JMU offers meal plans, and different locations may accept meal swipes, Dining Dollars, or other payment methods. Options and hours can change, so check the official meal plan information before choosing a plan or visiting a dining location.
