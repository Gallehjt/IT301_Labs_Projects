Student Life at JMU

College isn't just about school, it's also about meeting people, discovering new interests, and making memories. JMU students can meet friends and get involved in many ways.

400+ clubs and organizations

Things to Do at JMU

Join a Club or Organization

From academic and cultural groups to volunteer organizations and hobby clubs, there are lots of ways to meet people who share your interests. Student Org Night is the best place for new students to explore their options and find where they fit in.

Learn about JMU clubs and organizations

Enjoy Events and Activities

JMU's University Program Board (UPB) organizes events such as movies, concerts, and other campus activities. There's never a dull moment at JMU!

Explore University Program Board events

Stay Active with UREC

University Recreation (UREC) offers fitness activities, intramural sports, group exercise, and other recreational programs.

See what UREC offers

Show Your Duke Spirit

Students support the Dukes at sports games and campus events. Purple and gold are everywhere!

Read about JMU traditions

Getting Started

If you become a Duke, here are a few things you can try:

  1. Visit Student Org Night to explore clubs.
  2. Check the campus events calendar.
  3. Tour UREC and try a recreational activity.
  4. Attend a JMU event and wear purple!

You can also read about campus areas and dining options on this website.
